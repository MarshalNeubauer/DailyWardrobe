Daily backups

The server keeps the wardrobe as it was at the start of each day for 30 days, and the first of each month for a year. Rolling back replaces the wardrobe, history and settings on every device with that copy.

The server reads your photos, makes the realistic photos and keeps your backup. An invite link fills these in; saved when you tap Done.

Privacy

Your wardrobe lives on this device and, once you are connected, in your own backup on the server.

The server is run by the person who invited you. They can see everything kept there: your backup, your photos and the feedback you send, with your name. It runs on Cloudflare, and the app itself is served by GitHub Pages; both keep the usual short logs of visits.

The photos of your items are sent to Anthropic to be read (type, colour, pattern) and to OpenAI to make the outfit pictures.

Your weather location (the city you search for, or your phone’s position if you use it) is sent to Open-Meteo for the forecast. Public holidays come from Nager.Date, or Google’s holiday calendars for some countries; only the country is sent.

Nothing else leaves your phone. Delete my data, above, removes it from the server too.
