Sync

Your wardrobe is backed up to your Cloudflare Worker and kept in sync across your devices.

Daily backups

The worker keeps the wardrobe as it was at the start of each day for 30 days, and the first of each month for a year. Rolling back replaces the wardrobe, history and settings on every device with that copy.

The Cloudflare Worker that tags photos, makes the realistic photos and keeps your backup. Saved when you tap Done.

Privacy

Your wardrobe lives on this device and, once you are connected, in your own backup on the server.

The photos of your items are sent to Anthropic to be read (type, colour, pattern) and to OpenAI to make the outfit pictures.

Nothing else is shared with anyone.
